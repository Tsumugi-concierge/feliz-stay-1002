Equipment
Use the photos below to match the actual remote or panel in the room. The main instructions are shown as quick-use guides.

How to use A/C

Press COOL or HEAT to turn on A/C. Adjust temperature and fan volume with the buttons shown below.

COOL
HIGHER TEMP.
LOWER TEMP.
HEAT
STOP
AIR VOLUME
Note: Japanese homes typically do not have central heating systems. Air conditioners provide both cooling and heating but may take some time to fully adjust the room temperature.

If your remote has colored buttons

Use the blue button for COOL, orange for HEAT, yellow for DRY, purple for AUTO, and the large STOP button to turn it off.

Mitsubishi remote

Press Operation to start or stop. Use Mode Change, Temperature, and Fan Volume to adjust comfort.

Daikin remote

Choose AUTO, COOL, HEAT, DRY, or FAN, then adjust temperature and fan volume.

How to use Hot Water

  • Press the operation button to turn on hot water.
  • Use it before taking a bath or shower.
  • Turn it off when you are finished.
If there is no hot water, check that this controller is on before contacting support.

TV

Use the red power button at the top. Netflix, YouTube, Hulu, ABEMA, and Live TV have dedicated buttons.

Toilet Washlet

Press REAR or BIDET to activate the spray. Press STOP after use. The plus and minus buttons adjust water pressure.
